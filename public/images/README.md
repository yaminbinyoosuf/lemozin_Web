## Sources

Both photographs were downloaded from Pexels and cropped locally. They show
only women and are used under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

- `hero-driver-*` — Pexels photo 4429509, “Serious Asian woman driving right hand car”:
  <https://www.pexels.com/photo/serious-asian-woman-driving-right-hand-car-4429509/>
- `approach-*` — Pexels photo 4911489, “A Pair of Women Inside a Car Talking”:
  <https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-pair-of-women-inside-a-car-talking-4911489/>

The source files were resized with Pillow and encoded as WebP with `cwebp`
(quality 82). The hero is a 4:5 crop of the original; the intro image is a 4:3
crop.
